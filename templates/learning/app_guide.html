Using Snow School

Use this walkthrough during the supervisor introduction and before your first lesson. You can return here from the teaching hub or onboarding.

1. Complete your onboarding

Review your name, phone, address and emergency contact. Upload certification and required documents in Profile. Open onboarding to see what is complete, what needs review and which school documents require acknowledgment.

Importing a certification does not verify the document. Read the current revision before acknowledging a school SOP.

{% if current_user.user_type == 'instructor' %}Profile & documents · Onboarding progress{% else %}Staff onboarding administration{% endif %}

2. Review your winter availability

The season runs December 1, 2026–March 30, 2027. Fill a weekly pattern, then check individual dates and holidays. Select morning, afternoon and evening periods you can work and save the reviewed season. An imported pattern still needs your review.

Availability is a preference, not an assigned shift. Check ADP for your work schedule.

{% if current_user.user_type == 'instructor' %}Review availability{% else %}Staff availability{% endif %}

3. Find your lessons

Today groups your daily assignments into Classes, Privates and School Groups. Classes shows assignments across dates. Open the assigned group to view participants, take attendance and record coaching progress.

Support notes and shared contact numbers appear only when the school has authorised them for your assigned participant. Student email addresses are kept out of the instructor view.

{% if current_user.user_type == 'instructor' %}Open Today · All classes{% endif %}

4. Prepare and record progress

Open the released weekly training, review the teaching content and complete any requested acknowledgment. Your training acknowledgment and admin-recorded attendance are separate records.

Record observations for the correct participant and session. Families see issued evaluations and school-approved completion certificates.

Weekly training · Instructor manual

5. Capture photos and videos

Open media capture from the assigned lesson. Select every participant visible and check that consent is available. Capture inside the app, then upload. Captures are not added to your camera roll. Eligible families can view uploaded media through the parent portal.

Check the upload confirmation before leaving the screen. Follow your school’s media policy.

6. Check messages and get help

Check your notification inbox for school updates. Allow device notifications when prompted if you want push alerts.

For urgent incidents, contact the supervisor and ski patrol directly. Recording an incident in the app does not send an emergency alert.
