ADP: scheduling and payroll

ADP is the resort’s employee scheduling and payroll system. HR provides your employee number and access instructions.

  • Profile: review your details and add your photo.
  • Messages: check HR announcements and staff updates.
  • Pay statements: view available payment information.
  • Time card: review recorded clock-in and clock-out times; report discrepancies to your manager.
  • Schedule: view assigned shifts and the available time-off tools.

Snow School availability records your preferences; it does not replace your assigned ADP shifts.

The source text export did not include the ADP screenshots. This text guide replaces the missing image callouts; HR can provide current ADP screens and instructions.
