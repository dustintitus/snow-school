Snow School privacy policy

Updated October 23, 2026.

Snow School provides coaching and school administration tools. Snow School Coach is for coaches and staff; families and athletes access their school records through the website. Privacy questions can be sent to dustin.titus@gmail.com.

Information used by the service

School accounts include names, usernames, email addresses, roles and password hashes. Schools may maintain class assignments, attendance, session notes, skills observations, evaluations, progress reports, training records and incident information. Instructor records may include contact and address details, availability, certifications, background check documents, contracts, training clearances and versioned SOP acknowledgments. Administrators may share participant support notes and explicitly approved phone contacts with assigned instructors. Birthdates support participant lookup within authenticated, linked family accounts. When permitted, coaches may capture participant photos and silent videos directly into private school storage, subject to the service’s parent consent checks.

The notification service stores messages, recipients, read status, category preferences, and phone push tokens linked to staff accounts. Hosting services also process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser or device information, to operate and secure the service.

Why information is used

Information supports sign-in, authorized school operations, attendance, teaching and evaluation workflows, private record access, notifications, support and service security. The application does not include advertising or an advertising tracking SDK.

Access and service providers

Access to school records is controlled by account role, class assignments and family links. The service uses Vercel for hosting and private media and staff document storage, a hosted PostgreSQL database for school records, and Expo with Apple’s push service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for phone notification delivery. These providers process information needed to operate their services. Providers may process information outside your province or country.

Phone lock-screen alerts use generic text directing you to the authenticated inbox. Notification permission is optional. Disabling phone alerts does not remove messages from your inbox or prevent use of coaching tools.

Permissions and choices

Camera permission is used for in-app photo and silent video capture. Captures are uploaded directly and are not saved to the phone’s photo library by the app. The app does not request device location or contact-list access. You can change camera and notification permissions in your phone settings and notification preferences in the app.

Retention and requests

School records remain in the service until removed through an authorized school or support process. No automatic season-end deletion is currently implemented. If you want to know a school’s retention requirements or request access, correction, export or deletion, contact the school administrator or the support address above. Identity and authority must be verified before records can be disclosed or changed; school recordkeeping requirements may affect a request.

Security and updates

The service uses encrypted network connections, authenticated access and private media storage. Only authorized users should use school accounts; keep sign-in details private. We will update this page when service practices change.
