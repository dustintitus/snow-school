How participant media works

  • Authorized instructors may capture progress photos and silent videos directly inside the Snow School app.
  • Media is stored in private app storage and is visible only to authorized Snow School staff and linked family accounts.
  • It will not be posted publicly or used for advertising or social media through this acknowledgment.
  • In-app capture does not save captures to the instructor’s personal camera roll.
  • You can change your choice later or ask Snow School administration to remove stored media.
