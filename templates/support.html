Snow School Coach support

For app help, account questions or privacy requests, contact dustin.titus@gmail.com.

Getting started

Enter the school code provided by your snow school, then sign in with your coach or staff account. For Horseshoe Valley, use HORSESHOE. Family and athlete accounts use the school website.

Missing classes or evaluations?

Your school administrator manages class assignments, program schedules and evaluation weeks. Contact the school to correct those records.

Phone notifications

In the app options, open Notifications → Notification settings. Save your category preferences, enable Push Notifications, then allow phone alerts. If permission was denied, use Open phone settings. A saved inbox check and an actual phone banner are separate checks.

Privacy and account requests

Contact support or your school administrator to request access, correction, export or deletion of your account or records. Do not include passwords or sensitive learner information in an initial email.
